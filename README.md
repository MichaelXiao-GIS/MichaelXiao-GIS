# Hi, I’m Michael Xiao 👋

**PhD candidate in Social and Geographic Data Science · GeoDS · UCL Geography**

I’m a PhD candidate at University College London and a GIS and remote sensing engineer specialising in spatial data analysis and GeoAI applications. Alongside my research, I’m a postgraduate teaching assistant in UCL’s Department of Geography.

### Research interests

- **Historical GIS & geodemographics** — digitising and georeferencing historical records to study people and places over time.
- **Spatial data analysis** — geographic information systems, spatial data modelling and computational geography.
- **GeoAI & remote sensing** — deep learning, natural language processing and AI for geographic applications.
